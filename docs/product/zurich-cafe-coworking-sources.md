## Follow-up: Kraftwerk, Büro Züri and Auer & Co.

- [Büro Züri FAQ](https://buero-zueri.ch/faq): free reservation and use for up to one day, open to non-customers, BYOD, guest Wi-Fi with a mobile-number login, Mon–Fri 09:00–18:00 and Sat 09:00–17:00, closed on Sundays and public holidays. The official booking calendar requires sign-in; Lumen does not promise a desk. The [canton's statutory holidays](https://www.zh.ch/de/wirtschaft-arbeit/arbeitsbedingungen/arbeitsssicherheit-gesundheitsschutz/arbeits-ruhezeiten/feiertage.html) confirm the closing rule applies on Dec 25, Dec 26, and Jan 1. The [bank's own branch calendar](https://www.zkb.ch/de/lps/corporate/jahreswechsel.html) is not a coworking calendar. Leave Dec 24, Dec 31, and Jan 2 unknown rather than importing branch hours.
- [Kraftwerk's café calendar](https://kraftwerk.zuerich/cafe-bar/#feiertage) was read after its dynamic tables loaded: Mon–Tue close 20:00, Wed 22:00, Thu–Fri midnight; normal weekend service is closed; the winter closure is Dec 21–Jan 3. [The operator](https://kraftwerk.zuerich/about/) explicitly welcomes work on the outdoor terrace. There is no verified indoor laptop window, Wi-Fi, or stay limit. Show the café to check during open hours and exclude it during confirmed closures; do not treat an event booking as normal service.
- [Auer & Co.](https://www.auer.coffee/) publishes regular café hours and refers requests for quiet work to Impact Hub. [The Colab page](https://zurich.impacthub.ch/en/about-us/locations/colab-auer-co/) lists the café and coworking separately, and their public pages differ about Friday closing. No verified café-wide laptop policy or holiday calendar was found. Leave it unpublished.
