## Zürich expansion

- [ETH Library Reading Room](https://library.ethz.ch/en/about-us-and-locations/locations-and-opening-hours/reading-room.html) and [Architecture and Civil Engineering Library](https://library.ethz.ch/en/ueber-uns-und-standorte/locations-and-opening-hours/architecture-and-civil-engineering-library.html) publish separate regular hours and a [shared exception calendar](https://library.ethz.ch/en/about-us-and-locations/locations-and-opening-hours/special-opening-hours.html). The reading room has additional restrictions for Dies Academicus and Polyball. Both close from noon on December 24 through January 3.
- [ETH Library's visitor FAQ](https://library.ethz.ch/en/find-media/borrowing-and-using/frequently-asked-questions.html) confirms public access and guest Wi-Fi for personal devices. The reading room's three-hour research-terminal limit is not a verified personal-laptop stay limit. Coordinates and civic postal codes come from the federal address directory; ETH's institutional mailing postal codes differ.
- [PBZ Altstadt](https://www.pbz.ch/location/pbz-altstadt/) stays out of the verified set while renovation changes staffed and self-service access. Recheck the branch after the work ends.
